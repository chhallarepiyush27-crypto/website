The person behind the work

Curious about the physics. Driven to build.

I grew up in Mumbai and studied physics at Purdue, where experimental research taught me to stay with difficult questions. Today I’m pursuing an M.S. in Semiconductor Engineering at Northeastern University, with an interest in devices, fabrication, and circuit design.

I’m interested in the connection between physical principles and the hardware built around them—from quantum behavior to semiconductor devices and control circuits. Outside the lab, you can usually find me reading, playing chess, at the gym, or planning a trip.
