About me

The curiosity behind the work.

From physics experiments to semiconductor engineering, I’m learning how to move between fundamental questions and practical hardware.

Hello, I’m Piyush

A physicist drawn to making things work.

I grew up in Mumbai and graduated from Purdue University in 2026 with a B.S. in Physics and a certificate in Quantum Information Science and Technology. Working on quantum materials taught me to question a result, refine an experiment, and keep going when the answer is unclear.

I’m now studying Semiconductor Engineering at Northeastern University in Boston, concentrating in Devices & Nanosystems. My interests include semiconductor devices, fabrication, analog and RF circuits, and quantum hardware.

I value both the theory behind a physical effect and the hands-on work needed to measure and use it. That combination is shaping the research questions I pursue next.
