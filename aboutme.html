Hello, I’m Piyush

Physics as a foundation. Engineering as a direction.

I grew up in Mumbai and graduated from Purdue University in 2026 with a B.S. in Physics and a certificate in Quantum Information Science and Technology. Working on quantum materials taught me to question a result, refine an experiment, and keep going when the answer is unclear.

I’m now studying Semiconductor Engineering at Northeastern University in Boston, concentrating in Devices & Nanosystems. My focus spans quantum systems, semiconductor devices, and hardware engineering, including fabrication, analog and RF circuits, and digital logic.

As a Graduate Student Researcher in the Ebrahimi Lab, I’m modeling superconducting transmon dynamics and microwave control pulses. My longer-term research direction is to connect quantum-device physics, chip fabrication, and qubit-control RFIC design.

I value both the theory behind a physical effect and the hands-on work needed to measure and use it.
